# Abyssfire Unreal Rebuild — Binding Decisions

These answer every open question raised in `Docs/spec/*.md`. They are **binding** for all code, data and art.
Starting point (G1 overrides it): **the specs carry the web game's numbers and feel (balance, timings, formulas); fix
bugs; take the 3D/touch upgrade wherever the web version was limited by being 2D or web, and improve on it wherever a
decision below says so.** When a spec lists a "web quirk" with a recommendation and it is not decided otherwise below,
**follow the spec's recommendation**.

| ID | Principle |
|---|---|
| G1 | **The Unreal game is a remake; the web build is not a parity reference.** The specs in `Docs/spec/` and this file are the reference: nobody compares the Unreal game with the web game (`src/`) or copies its behaviour to settle a question. Where a spec describes web behaviour that a decision here replaces, the decision wins; new work may improve on the specs where a decision records it. (User, 2026-10.) |

Owner of the user-level product decisions: the user (3D oblique camera, no placeholder art, portable C++ core + UE
module, UE 5.8+, macOS first then Windows/Android/iOS with touch UI, Chapter 1 fully playable first).

## P — Platform and engine (ue58-platform.md)

| ID | Decision |
|---|---|
| P1 | Pin **UE 5.8** (`EngineAssociation "5.8"`, tested against 5.8.3+ hotfixes). C++20 everywhere. |
| P2 | Desktop: deferred + TSR (FXAA on low). Mac Apple Silicon Metal SM5; Windows D3D12 SM5. |
| P3 | Mobile: **forward + MSAA 4×** (opt out of 5.8 multi-pass deferred). Android **Vulkan only** (min API 26, target 36); iOS 17+. |
| P4 | UI: **Slate in C++**, ScaleToFit design 1280×720, safe zones. No UMG assets. Touch targets: the **touch HUD controls** (joystick, skill/potion/dodge/portal buttons, Talk/Use) are ≥ 44 pt; panels keep the 720-unit layout with the touch scale and ≥ 32 pt hit areas. A dedicated touch re-layout of panels is a follow-up. |
| P5 | Animation: no Animation Blueprint. Native `UAbyssAnimInstance` + custom proxy (crossfade + additive), timing owned by the core (spec §7 Option A). |
| P6 | VFX: code-driven pooled particles on ISM components + generated meshes/materials; no Niagara assets in milestone 1. |
| P7 | Outline: inverted hull **baked by Blender** into every character/prop mesh (second material slot). |
| P8 | Data: JSON in `unreal/Data`, staged UFS via `RuntimeDependencies`; UE reads bytes, the core parses. |
| P9 | One persistent map `L_Main`; zones built at runtime from map data. |
| P10 | **Shadows** *(revised by the art pass, 2026-10; was: CSM 2 cascades desktop, 1 cascade high-tier mobile, blobs on low/mid mobile and small props).* **Desktop, every tier:** CSM with **2 cascades** (review fix: was 3), `CascadeDistributionExponent` 1.0, shadow distance **1.35 × the live camera distance** (2870 cm at the default framing; it follows the zoom, re-fitted when the distance moves > 2 %). UE splits linearly from the near plane and at pitch −50 the visible ground lies at 66–90 % of that distance in view depth (1907–2573 cm at the default framing, the same ratio at every zoom): with 2 cascades all of it falls in the far one (0.55 cm per texel at 4096; a third cascade's last slice was 0.51 and the other two covered the air under the camera; 1 cascade would be 0.79, A/B with `r.Shadow.CSM.MaxCascades 1`). `r.Shadow.MaxCSMResolution` 2048 on low, 4096 on balanced / high, `r.Shadow.RadiusThreshold` 0, `r.Shadow.CSMShadowDistanceFadeoutFraction` 0.05; contact shadows (`ContactShadowLength` 0.03) on balanced / high. Sun shadow bias 0.6, slope bias 0.8 (tune for acne on the Mac). **Mobile:** no CSM on low / mid; high: 1 cascade at 2048 with the same distance rule. **Blob shadows under every character on every tier** (manifest `blobShadowRadiusCm`), drawn at 0.7 alpha wherever the sun CSM is on; small props keep blobs. The ink hull never casts. |
| P11 | **Toon = "shadow-catcher lit toon"; Substrate stays off** *(revised by the art pass, 2026-10; was: Unlit + custom lighting via a Material Parameter Collection, spec §6.4).* `M_AF_Toon` / `M_AF_Toon_Foliage` become **DefaultLit**: each computes today's toon colour (*lit*) and its shade-band floor (*shade*), then Emissive = shade + (1 − k)(lit − shade), BaseColor = k(lit − shade), Normal = MPC `KeyLightDir` (world space), Specular 0, Metallic 0, Roughness 1. The sun is **π lux × SunColor** (lighting channel 0 only): in full sun the output equals today's colours, in a cast shadow it drops to the shade band. The light keeps its colour as sRGB bytes and the renderer decodes them, so the sun is set with sRGB-encoded bytes and MPC `SunColor` is their decoded value (review fix: storing the linear colour as raw bytes decoded it twice — up to 48 % less per channel on toon and ground than the MPC said; the ground's sun term is now the intended SunColor, so re-check `abyss.SunScale` on the Mac). k = `CastShadowStrength`: characters 0.7, loot 0.8, props 1.0, canopies 0.6. Where there is no CSM (mobile low / mid) the result is identical to the old unlit look. The lit ground keeps its sun term through MPC `SunScale` (`abyss.SunScale` 0.55). **No engine local lights on toon:** torches, campfires and spell glows light toon surfaces through 8 (desktop) / 4 (mobile) MPC light slots written by `UAbyssVfxSystem`: Emit += base × colour × intensity × lightScale × 3.5 × (1 − d²/r²)² × wrap. The slime shell, fade ghosts, FX, ink and selection rim stay Unlit. Any future desktop point light must use lighting channel 1 (the fake normal would mis-light toon). The fake normal also means GBuffer-normal effects must skip toon pixels: the SSAO composite (P14) darkens the visible ground only. Revisit Substrate Toon (desktop only) once it leaves experimental. |
| P12 | Bundle ids: `com.feuvan.abyssfire` (iOS/Mac/Android). Apple team id and Play account are filled in by the user (left as config TODO with a clear message). |
| P13 | Gamepad: movement, attack, dodge, skills and pause menu in milestone 1; full UI focus navigation later. |
| P14 | **Post-process and atmosphere** (art pass, 2026-10). `M_AF_PP_Grade` is no longer bound (no exact web grade). Built-in grade at **55 % of the zone mood**: ColorGain = lerp(1, ambient, ambientAlpha × 0.5), saturation × 1.06 × (1 + (sat − 1) × 0.55), contrast 1 + (con − 1) × 0.55, lift / gain × 0.55 as shadow / highlight offsets, no global offset (the haze became the fog). **ToneCurveAmount 0.4** on every tier (`abyss.ToneCurve`; 0.6 dulled the white rim and the sword highlights in the look-dev); Film* defaults, BlueCorrection / ExpandGamut 0. Manual exposure (`abyss.ExposureBias`, starts at 0.263), recalibrated on the Mac so the palette mid-tones stay within ±2/255; local exposure contrast 1.0. Vignette and bloom unchanged; the grade is off on the low tier as before. **ExponentialHeightFog per zone mood** (`zone_moods.json` `port.fog`): plains #F2E8CC max opacity 0.22 (density 0.85), forest #8C86C8 0.32 (1.1), mountain #CEDCEE 0.30 (1.0), desert #FFDEA6 0.26 (0.9), abyss #6E2A40 0.35 (1.2). The density is the look-dev extinction (opacity 1 − 2^(−density × metres / 10) past the start, plains ≈ 0.20 at the top screen edge); UE `FogDensity` = density / ln 2 × `abyss.FogScale`, because UE's line integral carries a ln 2 (HeightFogCommon.ush `CalculateLineIntegralShared`, 5.6.1; FogDensity / 1000 per cm). `abyss.Fog 0` hides it. Fog start = camera distance + 1 m (the hero's rows are never fogged), height falloff 0.2, no directional inscattering, no volumetric fog; per-vertex on mobile. **SSAO** with `M_AF_PP_AOComposite` (0.25) on desktop balanced (1 level) / high (2 levels) only: intensity 1, radius 80, quality 50; none on low and mobile. The composite darkens **the visible ground only** (review fix): while it is bound the terrain alone renders custom depth and a pixel is ground where the scene depth reaches it (1 cm tolerance, 4 cm fade) — classic SSAO orients its hemisphere by the GBuffer normal, which on toon pixels is the constant KeyLightDir (P11), and the selection rim sits behind its figure; characters, props, foliage, ink and rim keep their colour. Mac: compare AO on / off (`r.AmbientOcclusionLevels 0`) and look at the ground round feet and props. Lumen, Nanite, VSM and MegaLights stay off: desktop-only experiments later, never dependencies. Every look variable (`abyss.ExposureBias`, `ToneCurve`, `Bloom`, `Fog`, `FogScale`, `InkScale`) applies live. |

## S — Simulation core (all specs)

| ID | Decision |
|---|---|
| S1 | **Fixed 60 Hz sim step** in the core, ms as `double`, one `SimClock` that never resets. The UE layer accumulates real time and steps the core 0–4 times per frame (spiral-of-death clamp). Rendering interpolates. |
| S2 | The sim clock **pauses** while the world is frozen (story cinematic, modal pickers, pause menu, dialogue with a quest card). Story/UI animations run on real time. Pending strikes resume after the freeze. (classes-stats-skills §19.1 D13 is binding.) |
| S3 | Seeded, **deterministic** RNG: own PRNG (xoshiro128** seeded by SplitMix), one stream per domain (combat, loot, ai, world, events). Tests inject seeds. Saves store stream states. |
| S4 | Units: **1 tile = 100 uu (1 m)**. Tile (col,row) → world X = col·100, Y = row·100, Z from terrain. Pixel-derived web constants convert with the specs' factors (36 px/tile for projectile & arrow timing, 45 px/tile for VFX size) — one constant table in the core, never duplicated. |
| S5 | **Hero ground speed is uniform**: `moveSpeed / 36` tiles/s (3.333 at 120) for click, hold, keyboard and stick. Keyboard/stick get a 90 ms ramp to full speed and 60 ms stop; click-move starts instantly. |
| S6 | Elite-kill slow motion = global time dilation that scales **both** sim dt and visuals (true slow-mo). |
| S7 | Clock domains in combat: pending monster strikes are core timers on the sim clock; they abort only if the attacker dies or is stunned, not because of a cinematic. |

| C13 | Floating combat text, kill rewards and quest progress popups (combat-feel §12, quests §5.8): the web draws them as world objects under `ZONE_CAMERA_ZOOM` 1.8, so their px **offsets, rises, drifts, sinks and stack rows are web world px × the live camera scale** (`SAbyssWorldLayer::WebPx` = projected tile diagonal at the hero / 64, ≈ 1.77 at the default framing, follows the zoom); font sizes stay screen px. Rewards fade with the web's `Power2` (= Cubic.Out on y and alpha together), not a quad rise with a quad-in fade, which kept them nearly opaque 3× longer. Port addition (placement, `SAbyssWorldLayer::PlaceFloatGroup`): the label floats one event batch spawns on one anchor, split in arrival order into groups that hold each kind once (a kill's +EXP / +G / +Embers; also status / pickup lines; two kills on one spot in one frame make two groups, the second placed above the first), keep their web layout among themselves and are lifted together, at spawn, until — sampled every 40 ms over the next 1.6 s with the last paint's camera — their ink boxes (measured width × 0.85 em + outline, plus a line height per extra line of a multi-line text; 2 px clearance) clear every other float that is still at least 0.2 opaque, including a hit number still rising through the spot (the killing blow's −30 → −58 px; ≈ 24 web px lift). Labels rise on decelerating curves and start at their fastest, so a group placed clear above an older one never crosses it; lift ≤ 96 web px. Hit numbers keep the spec's position and stacking and are painted after (above) the label floats, so a later number rising through a reward stays readable. (First playtest: unscaled offsets put +EXP on the loot labels; second playtest: cell-keyed reward rows let kills 0.2 tile or > 0.6 s apart overlap.) *Third Mac playtest:* label floats (rewards, status / pickup lines) are regular weight with a 1 px outline (a centred 2 px stroke is 1 px outside the glyph; bold with a 2 px Slate outline closed the counters of a 13 px "+18 EXP"); hit numbers stay bold. Painter text fades its outline with the fill (`MakeText` draws the font outline untinted, which left a black "+18 EXP" behind a faded fill), and a float under alpha .7 cuts the fill out of its outline (`bSeparateFillAlpha`) so the outline does not darken the fading glyph. |
| C14 | **Cast target rule** (classes-stats-skills §9.2–9.9, combat-feel §6.2–6.5 / §9.2; replaces the web's `preferredTarget` (nearest monster on the map), its retarget and FIX Q7, the ground-anchor fallback to another monster or the hero's feet, traps at the hero's feet, chain lightning around the hero and the piercing self-nova). **(1) Resolve once, at the press** (`CombatSystem::ResolveCastTarget`): (a) the tapped monster / the lock, if alive; (b) desktop mouse + keyboard: the attackable monster under the cursor (`CmdCastSkill::hoverTarget`), if alive; (c) the nearest alive monster within the **cast reach** `max(range + castAcquirePadTiles, castAcquireMinTiles)` = `max(range + 2, 8)` = 8 tiles for every shipped skill (data: `skill_rules.json targeting`). A (b)/(c) pick becomes the selection (lock + ring + `TARGET_CHANGED`), except for buffs, teleport and hero-centred novas, which only turn toward it, and except a pick the cast cannot reach (`outOfRange`: as the lock it would win every later press and hold the basic attack on a far monster); such a pick is not the player's own lock (a click / tap / target cycle, `CombatSystem::PlayerLock`). Touch: a tap is (a), otherwise (c). A buffered press keeps its target (resolved again without the hover only if it died). Without a lock the target ring (combat-feel §9.5) previews the (c) pick (nearest alive within `castAcquireMinTiles`) instead of the web's nearest aggro monster, and auto-battle acquires its lock with that same pick instead of the web's "nearest on the map, inside its own aggro range" (§9.4), then casts through the resolver — ring, press and auto-battle never disagree. **(2) Release at that target** (shape from the skill data, `SkillCastShape`): projectiles launch from the hero toward its release position and home on it; ground AoEs and traps land on its tile (clamped to the cast reach); cones and lines aim at it and always include it (multishot keeps its aoeRadius fan, piercing arrow its range); chain lightning starts on it and jumps to the nearest unhit monster within aoeRadius of the previous link (≤ `chainMaxLinks` 6); instant single-target skills hit it; buffs and novas need none. Reach: melee strikes and charge need it within `range + rangeSlackTiles` (1), every other shape aimed at a target (projectile, instant, ground AoE, trap, chain, cone, line) within the cast reach; beyond, the press logs `zone.combat.outOfRange`, spends nothing and is buffered — another monster is never substituted, and a ground AoE is never cast short of it. A ranged cast follows its living target wherever it went before the beat; a melee strike whiffs if it stepped out of reach; if it died, ground / trap / cone / line casts keep aiming at its last position and the others resolve again (within their reach, no hover); a press that had no target keeps its no-target aim through the beat (a monster stepping into reach does not capture it). **(3) No target**: buffs and novas cast; ground AoEs land at the desktop cursor point (clamped to the cast reach), else `min(range, noTargetGroundAheadTiles 3)` ahead; traps at the cursor point, else the hero's feet; projectiles fly along the cursor direction (else the facing) for the cast reach and fizzle (`EvProjectileEnded.hit = false`, no impact shake); chains, cones and lines fire along it; melee / charge / instant single-target skills log `zone.combat.noTarget`; teleport keeps its cursor / stick destination, else the player's own lock (never a hover / auto-pick / auto-battle lock: an escape blink must not land on the monster just shot at), else 6 ahead (C8), and no longer clears the lock. **(4) Presentation**: the cast animation turns the hero toward the target, else the no-target aim point (teleport: its aimed destination); `EvSkillVfx` carries the release point(s) with the monster per point (`pointTargets`), the cast direction (`dir`) and the release sim time (`atMs`: one host frame dispatches up to 4 steps' events); projectile skills also emit their cast-glint `EvSkillVfx`; the selection uses the existing ring. UE draws exactly that: the hero's attack / cast face lock finishes its turn by half the Contact / Release offset (≤ 100 ms) instead of the M11 rate, and after a skill blink follows the core facing (shadow step faces its target on landing); `EvSkillVfx.dir` is the blow of cones, lines, bolts and chains; multishot fans its volley evenly over the core's `coneDeg` and sends one presentation-only homing arrow per hit monster that leaves at `atMs` and lands on its `SkillArrowDelayMs`; chain links (`points` anchors) follow the monster per point; projectile heads home on the live target and turn with their flight (a target-less shot flies to `to` and plays `.fizzle`); `origin` layers stay on the event origin when the caster is not there (a blink's departure, a trap / ground-effect centre). |
